FIPS Lab

A chaos lab for FIPS, a mesh network whose nodes are identified by Nostr keys. It runs a mesh in Docker, breaks it on purpose, and measures how it heals. 111 measured cuts turned up a five-minute window in which traffic can get stuck in a routing loop, which a Bayesian model then pinned down from the outside.

    FIPS Lab · 01

    Thirty seconds of silence

    How fast a mesh routes around a cut link. It usually takes the dead-link timeout plus a second, but in the first five minutes after start-up, replies can loop between two nodes until their hop limit runs out. I traced this to an anti-poisoning rule in the FIPS source.

  • Oct 2026
    FIPS Lab · 02

    A timer through the wall

    A censored mixture model in PyMC finds the same timer from the measurements alone: 301.4 ± 1.3 s, against 300 s in the source. I then used the model to predict the outcome of new cuts and checked the predictions in a separate run.

About

I have worked as a data scientist and as a software developer. The work I enjoy most combines the two: building a system and then measuring whether it does what it is supposed to do.

I am interested in sovereign technology, such as mesh networks without a coordinator and identities based on keys that no company controls. I test claims about such systems the way I would test a statistical model, with experiments and with predictions that could turn out to be wrong.
